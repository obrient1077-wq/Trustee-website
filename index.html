Caring for Catholic Education in Burlington

Tim O’Brien has served as a Catholic School Trustee, educator, and parish volunteer in Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5. His focus is on student well‑being, strong community connections, and responsible stewardship of Catholic education.

About Tim O’Brien

Tim O’Brien has lived and worked in Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5 for most of his professional life. As a drama and music teacher in Halton, a former Catholic School Trustee, and an active parish volunteer, he has been closely connected to local schools, parishes, and neighbourhoods.

Qualifications

  • Served four years as Catholic School Trustee in Burlington (city‑wide representation).
  • Served on CPIC, SAL, and the Discipline Committee as Trustee.
  • Brought forward and passed a motion for Halton Catholic to go paperless.
  • Initiated outreach to homeless individuals in Burlington’s downtown core.
  • Organized an annual parish/school talent show for students of a local Catholic school.
  • Served one year with the Lay Scarboro Foreign Mission Program.
  • Taught seventeen years in Halton as a drama and music teacher.
  • Served two years on a parish finance committee.
  • Initiated accessibility for the hearing‑impaired at a local Catholic church.
  • Served in several music ministries across Burlington: St. Philippe, St. John the Baptist, St. Paul the Apostle, St. Patrick, and St. Raphael.
  • Grand Knight for the Knights of Columbus.

Platform

Why I am running for HCDSB Trustee, Wards 4 & 5

I am running because I care about our schools. From recruiting for the Basketball Free Throw to playing for school Masses to volunteering in kindergarten classrooms, I have been engaged in the care of Ward 4 and 5 students—both during my time as Trustee and afterwards.

Priorities as a Trustee

My priority is the well‑being of students. They should be included, cared for, and supported in reaching their greatest potential. I also value meaningful community engagement: communication between home, school, and parish is essential. I will work to ensure staff have the professional tools they need to support students effectively.

Connection to Wards 4 & 5

I have lived in Wards 4 and 5 for most of my professional life and have volunteered in their schools. I am an associate member of the neighbourhood communities and have played organ and guitar in the churches of Wards 4 and 5. It is where I live, and I am proud of it.

Wards 4 & 5

Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5 include schools, parishes, and neighbourhoods that Tim has served for many years. His work in classrooms, parishes, and community outreach has been rooted in these wards.
