Serving Catholic Education in Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5

Tim O’Brien has served as a Catholic School Trustee, educator, and parish volunteer in Burlington. His work focuses on student well‑being, strong home–school–parish relationships, and responsible stewardship of Catholic education.

Support Tim O’Brien

Tim O’Brien has lived and worked in Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5 for most of his professional life. As a drama and music teacher in Halton, a former Catholic School Trustee, and an active parish volunteer, he has been closely connected to local schools, parishes, and neighbourhoods.

Experience & Service

Trustee Leadership

  • Four years as Catholic School Trustee in Burlington (city‑wide).
  • Served on CPIC, SAL, and Discipline Committee.
  • Brought forward and passed motion for Halton Catholic to go paperless.

Education & Outreach

  • Seventeen years teaching drama and music in Halton.
  • Initiated outreach to homeless in Burlington’s downtown core.
  • Organized annual parish/school talent show for Catholic students.

Parish & Mission Work

  • Served with Lay Scarboro Foreign Mission Program (one year).
  • Two years on parish finance committee.
  • Initiated hearing‑accessibility at a local Catholic church.

Music Ministry & Knights

  • Music ministry at St. Philippe, St. John the Baptist, St. Paul the Apostle, St. Patrick, and St. Raphael.
  • Grand Knight, Knights of Columbus (St. Philippe French Catholic Church).

Platform

Why I am running for HCDSB Trustee, Wards 4 & 5

I am running because I care about our schools. From recruiting for the Basketball Free Throw to playing for school Masses to volunteering in kindergarten classrooms, I have been engaged in the care of Ward 4 and 5 students—both during my time as Trustee and afterwards.

Priorities as a Trustee

My priority is the well‑being of students. They should be included, cared for, and supported in reaching their greatest potential. I also value meaningful community engagement: communication between home, school, and parish is essential. I will work to ensure staff have the professional tools they need to support students effectively.

Connection to Wards 4 & 5

I have lived in Wards 4 and 5 for most of my professional life and have volunteered in their schools. I am an associate member of the neighbourhood communities and have played organ and guitar in the churches of Wards 4 and 5. It is where I live, and I am proud of it.

Wards 4 & 5

Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5 include schools, parishes, and neighbourhoods that Tim has served for many years. His work in classrooms, parishes, and community outreach has been rooted in these wards, with a focus on supporting families and students in Catholic education.
