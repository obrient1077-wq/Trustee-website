Serving Catholic Education in Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5

Tim O’Brien has served as a Catholic School Trustee, educator, and parish volunteer in Burlington. His work focuses on student well‑being, strong home–school–parish relationships, and responsible stewardship of Catholic education.

About Tim

Tim O’Brien has lived and worked in Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5 for most of his professional life. As a drama and music teacher in Halton, a former Catholic School Trustee, and an active parish volunteer, he has been closely connected to local schools, parishes, and neighbourhoods.

Experience & Service

Trustee Leadership

  • Four years as Catholic School Trustee in Burlington.
  • Served on CPIC, SAL, and Discipline Committee.
  • Introduced paperless initiative at HCDSB.

Education & Outreach

  • Seventeen years teaching drama and music in Halton.
  • Outreach to homeless in Burlington’s downtown core.
  • Organized annual parish/school talent show.

Parish & Mission Work

  • Lay Scarboro Foreign Mission Program (one year).
  • Two years on parish finance committee.
  • Hearing‑accessibility improvements at a local church.

Music Ministry & Knights

  • Music ministry at multiple Burlington parishes.
  • Grand Knight, Knights of Columbus (St. Philippe).

Key Priorities

Student Well‑Being

Supporting students in reaching their potential through inclusion, care, and strong relationships between home, school, and parish.

Community Engagement

Encouraging meaningful communication and collaboration among families, staff, and parish communities.

Responsible Stewardship

Ensuring resources are used effectively to support student learning and professional tools for staff.

Wards 4 & 5

Burlington’s Wards 4 & 5 include schools, parishes, and neighbourhoods that Tim has served for many years. His work in classrooms, parishes, and community outreach has been rooted in these wards.
